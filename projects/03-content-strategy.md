# Project 03 — Content Strategy & 14-Day Campaign

**Brand:** LunchLoop (fictional Nigerian meal-prep subscription concept)  
**Project type:** Independent practice case study  
**Status:** Content proposal; not published

## 1. Objective

Create a practical content plan that helps a new meal-prep concept explain its value, answer common questions and invite interested people to join a waitlist.

## 2. Content pillars

1. **Convenience:** Make weekday meal planning feel easier.
2. **Practical education:** Meal planning, budgeting and food storage tips.
3. **Product clarity:** Explain the proposed service, menu, pricing and service area honestly.
4. **Community:** Ask questions and learn what potential customers need.
5. **Trust:** Share real process information and verified feedback only when available.

## 4. Sample copy

**Hook:** Your lunch break is short. Deciding what to eat shouldn't take most of it.

**Body:** We're exploring a simpler weekday meal routine for busy people. What matters most to you: convenience, price, variety or predictable planning?

**CTA:** Tell us below. Your feedback will help shape the concept.

## 5. Measurement plan

- Reach and video completion: awareness
- Saves and shares: usefulness
- Replies and poll responses: audience insight
- Profile and landing-page visits: interest
- Waitlist sign-ups: conversion

Review performance weekly. Compare content by its objective; do not treat likes or views as proof of sales.

## 6. Accessibility and quality checks

- Use readable text and captions on videos.
- Add descriptive alt text to informative images.
- Keep calls to action specific.
- Verify prices, ingredients and service claims before publishing.
- Get permission before sharing customer photos or testimonials.

**Learning demonstrated:** content planning, copywriting, audience engagement, campaign measurement and responsible communication.
