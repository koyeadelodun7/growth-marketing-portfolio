# Project 01 — Launch & User Acquisition Strategy

**Brand:** LunchLoop (fictional Nigerian meal-prep subscription concept)  
**Project type:** Independent practice case study  
**Status:** Strategy proposal; not launched

## 1. Brief

LunchLoop is a hypothetical service offering convenient, pre-planned weekday meals to busy students and early-career professionals. The challenge is to introduce the service with a limited marketing budget and learn which audience and message generate the strongest early demand.

## 2. Audience hypotheses

| Segment | Need / pain point | Message to test |
|---|---|---|
| Busy students | Affordable, convenient meals between classes | Make your busy day easier |
| Early-career professionals | Less time to plan lunch during workdays | Spend less time figuring out lunch |
| Shared-apartment residents | Predictable meals and simple group ordering | Make weekday meals simpler together |

These are hypotheses to validate through interviews, polls and landing-page behaviour—not verified market findings.

## 3. Positioning hypothesis

**For busy people who want a simpler weekday lunch routine, LunchLoop is a meal-prep concept that makes planning and ordering convenient.**

Before using this positioning publicly, validate willingness to pay, delivery feasibility, food preferences and trust requirements.

## 4. Proposed acquisition funnel

1. **Awareness:** Short-form videos, student/community groups and micro-creators.
2. **Interest:** A simple landing page explaining the offer, menu concept and service area.
3. **Consideration:** Sample menu, transparent pricing, FAQs and customer feedback once available.
4. **Conversion:** A clear waitlist or first-order call to action.
5. **Retention:** Post-purchase feedback, menu updates and opt-in reminders.

## 5. Low-cost experiments

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Measurement | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two message variants | Convenience-led copy may outperform price-led copy | Landing-page sign-up rate | Keep the clearer winner after a fair test |
| Short menu video vs. static menu | Video may generate more qualified interest | Link clicks and sign-ups | Compare by reach and conversion, not views alone |
| Community post with waitlist link | Relevant communities may drive qualified leads | Sign-ups by tagged link | Repeat only in communities with permission and meaningful interest |

## 6. Metrics to monitor

- Landing-page visits
- Waitlist or first-order conversion rate
- Cost per qualified lead
- First-order conversion
- Repeat purchase rate (once orders exist)

## 7. Risks and considerations

- Do not promise delivery coverage until operations can support it.
- Confirm food safety, pricing and fulfilment before taking orders.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Separate reach and engagement from actual customer acquisition.

## 8. What I would do next

Interview 8–12 people across the proposed segments, document recurring needs, test two landing-page messages and use the findings to choose one initial audience.

**Learning demonstrated:** audience segmentation, positioning, funnel thinking, experiment design and KPI selection.
