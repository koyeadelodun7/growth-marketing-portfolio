# Project 02 — Community & Referral Growth Plan

**Brand:** LunchLoop (fictional Nigerian meal-prep subscription concept)  
**Project type:** Independent practice case study  
**Status:** Programme proposal; not launched

## 1. Objective

Explore whether trusted community members can introduce a new meal-prep service to relevant audiences more efficiently than broad, untargeted promotion.

## 2. Programme concept

Recruit a small pilot group of community representatives from student associations, co-working spaces and relevant local communities. Representatives share approved information and a unique tracking link; they do not make claims about service quality or delivery that have not been verified.

## 3. Ambassador selection

Look for people who:
- Are active and trusted in a relevant community.
- Communicate clearly and responsibly.
- Understand the audience's needs.
- Are willing to follow brand and disclosure guidelines.

## 4. Pilot workflow

1. Define the pilot location and confirm operational capacity.
2. Invite and screen a small group of interested representatives.
3. Provide a short product guide, FAQs and approved content.
4. Assign each representative a unique referral code or tagged URL.
5. Review results weekly and collect feedback.
6. Continue, adjust or stop based on qualified conversions and cost.

## 5. Incentive options to test

- A clearly disclosed reward for a verified first purchase.
- A customer referral benefit, subject to margin and fulfilment checks.
- Non-cash recognition for helpful feedback and responsible promotion.

Rewards should be paid only for valid actions, with clear terms and a process for handling duplicate or fraudulent referrals.

## 6. Measurement

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Active representatives | Shows programme participation |
| Visits by referral link | Measures traffic generated |
| Qualified sign-ups / orders | Measures useful acquisition |
| Conversion by representative | Identifies effective channels |
| Cost per acquired customer | Checks economic sustainability |
| Repeat purchase rate | Checks whether acquired customers stay |

## 7. Risks and safeguards

- Obtain permission before posting in groups or using community channels.
- Require clear disclosure when a representative is rewarded.
- Protect customer information and restrict access to tracking data.
- Do not reward spam, misleading claims or unverified sign-ups.
- Check that the business can fulfil demand before scaling the programme.

## 8. Next step

Run a small, time-limited pilot in one community, review the quality and cost of acquired customers, then decide whether to expand.

**Learning demonstrated:** partnership thinking, community-led acquisition, referral mechanics, measurement and ethical promotion.
